An individual user belonging to a Client, with their own login, who submits task requests and views the fleet assigned to them. May also make a Correction to a unit on any Contract of their own Client's Fleet: a SIM's number, a Postpaid SIM's plan, a Smartphone's model or its owner — not its serial, and never a Carrier.
**Owner** (of a Smartphone or SIM Card):
A Smartphone is owned either by the Client or by the company; a SIM Card is always company-owned. A Smartphone reached through a Provision or Replace Request arrives company-owned, and may be corrected afterwards. The owner decides a Return's path. A Manager or a Tester of the Contract's own Client may correct it in either direction, refused (409 `SMARTPHONE_ON_OPEN_RETURN`) while a Return naming the phone is Pending Approval, Submitted or In Progress, and allowed again once that Return is Compl

**Correction**:
Fixing a mistake on a unit already on a Contract's Fleet — a SIM's number, a Postpaid SIM's plan, a Smartphone's model or its owner — in place, without raising a Request. Made by the Company Manager or a Client Tester, never an Agent; refused with a clear message when it would break a rule, and audit-logged with who made it, the old value and the new value. Not a Request, and not a change of Carrier or of Postpaid/Prepaid.
**Carrier**:
A mobile operator in one country, offering **Topup Options** (for a Topup Request) and **Postpaid Plans** (for a Postpaid SIM), each with a name and a price in that country's currency. Maintained by the Agents of that country, who know their local carriers' offers; archived rather than deleted once in use.
