## Question

If a credit is **larger than the invoice** it lands on (a $300 credit on a $250 invoice), what does that invoice show?

**Recommendation:** A total of −$50.00, with nothing carried to the following month. It's rare and honest, and splitting it across months can be added later without touching stored data.
